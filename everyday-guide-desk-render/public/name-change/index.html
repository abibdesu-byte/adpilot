Before you call

A few helpful answers.

Are you a government office?

No. Everyday Guide Desk is an independent information service. Official applications, changes, and eligibility decisions remain with the relevant government agency or organization.

Can you change my name or get my certificate?

No. We can explain published guidance and help you locate the appropriate official office. We do not issue certificates, change records, or guarantee application outcomes.

Do I need to share private documents?

No documents or account passwords are needed for general guidance. Do not share a Social Security number, payment card details, or account login credentials with the help desk.

Can I also ask about life insurance?

Yes. If you are interested, tell the representative. They can offer an introduction to a licensed life insurance agent and ask your permission before transferring you. Having existing coverage does not prevent you from asking. Insurance information is separate from your name-change question.
