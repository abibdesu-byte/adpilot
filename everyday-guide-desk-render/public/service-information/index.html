Everyday Guide Desk

What to expect.

Everyday Guide Desk is an independent information service for general name-change questions after marriage and wedding-officiant questions.

General information

We explain general concepts and help callers locate published instructions or prepare questions for the appropriate organization. Our information is not a substitute for official requirements, legal advice, or instructions from a provider handling your situation.

Independent service

We are not a government agency, law firm, wedding officiant, venue, or officiant booking agency. We do not issue documents, submit government applications, change account records, book ceremonies, or guarantee eligibility, availability, or outcomes.

Optional life insurance information

You may ask for an introduction to a licensed life insurance agent. This conversation is optional and separate from your original inquiry. The help desk should identify the receiving business and obtain your permission before transferring the call. You can express interest even if you already have life insurance.

Fees and provider terms

The website does not take payments. Before accepting any service, ask the representative or provider to explain any applicable fees, terms, and business relationships. Government agencies and independent providers set their own charges and requirements.
