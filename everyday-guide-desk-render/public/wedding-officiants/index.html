Before you call

Good questions,
clear expectations.

Are you an officiant or a booking agency?

No. Everyday Guide Desk is an independent information service. We help explain general options and prepare questions. Contact your chosen officiant directly for services, availability, quotes, and bookings.

Can you guarantee an officiant for my date?

No. Availability, pricing, deposits, and service terms must be confirmed directly with the provider. We do not make reservations or take booking payments.

Can you explain the local marriage requirements?

We can point you toward official information. The rules vary by jurisdiction, and the responsible local office should confirm requirements for your situation. We do not provide legal advice.

Why might life insurance come up?

Planning a household can bring up financial-protection questions. If you would like, you can ask for an introduction to a licensed life insurance agent. It is optional, and the representative will ask permission before transferring you. You can express interest whether or not you already have coverage.
