Service information

Privacy.

This website provides information and links that open your phone’s dialer. It does not have a lead form, upload facility, customer account, or payment checkout.

Website visits

We have not installed advertising pixels, analytics cookies, or third-party analytics scripts on these pages. Our hosting provider, Render, may process ordinary request information, such as IP addresses and browser details, to deliver and protect the website.

Calling the help desk

When you choose to call, your phone provider and the receiving phone service handle the call and may receive caller ID and call metadata. Any recording notice or further information about how a call is handled should be provided by the representative. Ask before sharing personal information.

Keep sensitive information private

General guidance does not require Social Security numbers, login passwords, payment card details, or copies of identity documents. Do not send these to the help desk.

Optional insurance introductions

If you request an introduction to a licensed life insurance agent, the representative should identify the receiving business, explain what information will be shared, and ask your permission before transferring you. The receiving business will have its own privacy practices.

External resources

Official resource links take you to other websites. Those websites have their own privacy notices and service terms.

Contact

For privacy questions, call (575) 577-6846. Help-desk hours: 11 AM–8 PM EST.
